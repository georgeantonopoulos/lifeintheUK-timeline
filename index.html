British History Timeline

Stone Age
First people lived in Britain as hunter-gatherers.
About 6,000 years ago
First farmers arrived in Britain.
43 AD – 410 AD
Roman rule in Britain, including the building of Hadrian's Wall.
1066
Norman Conquest and Battle of Hastings. Start of the Middle Ages.
1215
Magna Carta.
1348
Black Death.
1400
English became the preferred language of courts and official documents.
1485
End of the Middle Ages.
1500s
Act for the Government of Wales; Elizabethan period.
1588
Defeat of the Spanish Armada.
1603
Union of the Crowns (James VI of Scotland becomes James I of England).
1640
Beginning of the English Civil War.
1660
The Restoration of the monarchy.
1665
Great Plague.
1679
Habeas Corpus Act.
1688
Glorious Revolution.
1689
Bill of Rights.
1707
Act of Union (England and Scotland) creates Kingdom of Great Britain.
1700s
The Enlightenment period.
1776
American colonies declare independence.
1800
Act of Union with Ireland creates the UK of Great Britain and Ireland (effective 1801).
1805
Battle of Trafalgar.
1815
Battle of Waterloo.
1832
Reform Act.
1833
Emancipation Act (abolished slavery).
1837 – 1901
Victorian Age (Queen Victoria's reign).
1853 – 1856
Crimean War.
1889 – 1902
Boer War.
1900s (general)
Period of significant inventions.
Before WWI
State retirement pension and free school meals introduced.
1913
Home Rule proposed for Ireland.
1914 – 1918
First World War.
1916
Easter Rising; Battle of the Somme.
1918
Women aged 30+ get the vote; WWI ends (11/11).
1921
Peace treaty splits Ireland.
1928
Women aged 21 get the vote; Penicillin discovered.
1929
Great Depression begins.
1939 – 1945
Second World War.
1940
Evacuation of Dunkirk; Battle of Britain.
1942
Beveridge Report.
1944
Education Act.
1945
WWII ends.
1945 – 1950
NHS and social security system established (NHS established 1948).
1947
9 colonies gain independence.
1982
Argentina invaded Falkland Islands.
2024
Labour government formed.
